# Media and redistribution

## Original audio library

126 original audio files were supplied by the project owner. On 2026-10-23 the owner explicitly confirmed the right to public redistribution and requested inclusion of the complete library. Supporting license documents/source references have not yet been provided. This is an owner authorization statement, not an independent license verification.

Audio is not relicensed under this repository's MIT license and is not represented as CC0. Original creators' license terms continue to apply. Do not infer unrestricted commercial reuse, standalone resale or sublicensing from the code license. Supporting documents should be added here when provided.

## Backgrounds

Nine original backgrounds were supplied by the project owner and included at their explicit request. Their provenance is recorded as owner-supplied; the repository does not claim independent authorship verification. They are not client photographs or private portraits.

## Fonts

Jersey 10, Press Start 2P and Silkscreen are accompanied by their official Google Fonts OFL license texts in assets/fonts/licenses/. Each font retains its own copyright and license terms.

## Integrity and exclusions

assets/media-manifest.json records the exact path, byte size and SHA-256 of every included media file. Private portraits/avatars, client campaigns, credentials, caches, generated production history and account connections are excluded. The code's MIT license applies to original code, not to third-party media or trademarks.
