# Intake

For a new film, record five brief categories in `intake.json`: goal, audience, visual direction, audio direction and constraints. Fill known answers from the user's request or supplied brief; ask only missing decisions, together, with a few concrete options. Do not re-ask five questions just to satisfy a template. Record actual answers and their source; never invent preferences or approval. If the user explicitly waives intake, record their exact wording in `user_waiver`; do not infer a waiver from silence.

Read `skills/creative-director/SKILL.md` for art-direction choices and the appropriate mode. Before a substantial build with an undecided visual direction, present two or three genuinely different proposals: dominant image, typography, palette, motion behavior and where each belongs in the film or page. A small styleboard or representative frame helps when words are insufficient. Revisions reuse the approved direction.

Website briefs cover the same decisions adapted to the medium (main action, audience, visual reference, real content and implementation constraints). Websites do not use the video job schema. The skill's references explain the distinction.
