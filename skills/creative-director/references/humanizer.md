# Humanizer: clear copy, captions and narration

Edit for a particular audience and medium. Preserve technical meaning, names, dates, uncertainty, citations and the user's voice. Do not simulate a personal experience, customer quote or result that was not supplied. This is an editorial pass, not an AI-detector evasion technique.

## A short editing pass

1. Identify the sentence's actual claim or instruction. Delete throat-clearing that delays it.
2. Replace abstract nouns and adjective piles with a concrete action, object or consequence.
3. Check whether any number, comparison, guarantee or superlative needs evidence. Remove or qualify unsupported claims; never make up specificity.
4. Vary sentence length naturally. Preserve needed terminology rather than replacing every word with a synonym.
5. Read aloud for narration. Resolve awkward clusters and abbreviations, then recheck timing against the actual recorded voice.
6. Check how the text wraps and where captions break. A sentence that reads well in a document may need a shorter screen version.

| Odkryj rewolucyjne możliwości kompleksowej automatyzacji. | Połącz formularz z kalendarzem. Nowe zgłoszenia trafią do wybranego terminu. |

| W dzisiejszym dynamicznym świecie oszczędność czasu jest kluczowa. | Raport wymaga dziś danych z trzech plików. Zobacz, jak je połączyć. |
| Unlock seamless productivity with a powerful all-in-one platform. | Review incoming requests and assign the next action in one queue. |
| Take your content to the next level. | Turn the approved script into editable scenes and a reviewable draft. |

These are editing examples, not facts to paste into an unrelated product. Keep a deliberate expressive line when it suits the brand; plain language does not require every sentence to sound identical.

## Medium-specific choices

- **Web:** headings tell the reader what the section offers; button text names the action and expected result. Avoid invented urgency and ambiguous promises.
- **Narration:** write for breathing, emphasis and pronunciation. Let visuals carry visible details instead of narrating every label.
- **Captions:** use accurate words, phrase-level breaks and readable holds. Do not add flashy emphasis to every word.
- **Social copy:** lead with the concrete subject or useful observation. Use a hook that the content actually resolves; do not create a false reveal.

Keep the language requested by the user. In Polish, check inflection, natural word order and names spoken by the voice engine. In English, check register and clarity rather than forcing marketing idioms. Report substantive factual changes separately from stylistic edits.
